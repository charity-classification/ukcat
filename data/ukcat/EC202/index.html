Water EC202¶

Water is a tag in the UK-CAT classification of charities in the UK. It is part of the Economic and community development categoryand the Infrastructure subcategory.

343 charities have been tagged with this tag. This represents 0.17% of all charities.

Keywords to find charities¶

UK-CAT categories are applied to charities based on a set of keywords, in the form of a regular expression.

The regular expression used to find charities in this tag is:

\b(water suppl(y|ies)+|supply of water|sewage|sanitation|saniti[sz]ation|water (purification|conservation|infrastructure)+|water pumps?)\b
